# Fresh chronological review: Books 129--188

## Review standard

This audit applies `AUTHORING.md` result by result. A book receives a final verdict only after a complete line-by-line read from line 1 through EOF, with substantive imported facts checked against an earlier manuscript or the local mathematical library. Forward references are classified as harmless roadmap prose only when they do no proof work. Named conditions that assert missing mathematical results are not treated as ordinary data hypotheses.

Status labels:

- **PASS**: fully read to EOF; no unresolved prerequisite or local defect found.
- **PASS WITH CROSS-BOOK FINDING**: fully read to EOF; local exposition is coherent, but a stated result depends on an unsupplied earlier theorem recorded below.
- **PENDING FULL READ**: inventory or mechanical checks may have been run, but the mandatory complete read has not yet been finished.
